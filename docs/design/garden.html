Question 3 · Objective 2.3
The next shot starts with a door slam. You want the viewer to hear it first. Which cut do you use?
L-cut · your answer
Audio from the first shot trails over the second. The sound follows, it does not lead.
J-cut · correct
The audio of the next shot starts early. The viewer hears the slam first.
Jump cut
A jump cut removes time inside one shot. It does not move audio.
