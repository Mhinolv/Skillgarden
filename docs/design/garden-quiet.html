What changed from Garden

  1. Surfaces are white and off-white. The course accent only tints the sidebar (4%) and the current row.
  2. Accent fill is only on the one primary action: Resume, Mark as Complete or Check answer.
  3. Progress uses one stone per section, plus "2 of 22 done". The per-lecture dots are gone.
  4. The objective is a light tinted line, not a solid green banner.
  5. "Recommended next" is a bordered "Up next" row, not a black bar.
  6. Secondary actions are small bordered or plain buttons, 28 px high, no 3D base.
  7. Type is smaller and lighter: 24 px title at weight 600 (was 34-44 heavy). Radii are 5-8 (was 14-32).
  8. The sidebar opens only the current section. Rows are one line, 26 px. "Course overview" is a plain row.
