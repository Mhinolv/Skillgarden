# Start seedlings

Sow tomato seeds 6 millimeters deep in small pots.

Keep the pots at 24 degrees until the seeds come up. This takes about 7 days.

Give the seedlings 14 hours of light each day.

## Harden off

One week before you plant them in the beds, put the seedlings outside for 2 hours a day. Add one hour each day.

## Transplant

Plant each seedling so that the soil covers the stem up to the first leaves.

Leave 60 centimeters between plants.
