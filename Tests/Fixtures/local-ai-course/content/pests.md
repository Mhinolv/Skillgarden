# Stop greenhouse pests

Aphids are small green insects. They live under the leaves.

To make the aphid spray, mix 5 milliliters of soap into one liter of water. Spray the leaves in the evening.

Yellow sticky cards catch whiteflies. Hang one card for each 2 square meters of the greenhouse.

## Ladybugs

Ladybugs eat aphids. Put 50 ladybugs on each bed after sunset, when they do not fly away.
