# Prepare the soil

Tomatoes grow best in loose soil with a pH between 6.0 and 6.8.

Mix one part compost with two parts garden soil for each bed.

Fill each bed to a depth of 30 centimeters.

Test the pH of the soil once each spring with a test kit.

## Mulch

Put a layer of straw 5 centimeters thick on the soil. The straw keeps the soil moist.

## Crop rotation

Do not plant tomatoes in the same bed two years in a row. Plant beans in that bed the next year.
